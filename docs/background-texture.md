# Jemná textura pozadí

Vytvořeno vestavěným nástrojem imagegen. Pro web převedeno do WebP bez změny rozměrů.

Výsledný podklad: `public/assets/textures/smoked-wood-flour.webp`.
Kontrast a odstín tlumí CSS; originální fotografie poboček i fotografie jídel zůstávají samostatné.

## Finální prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: seamless subtle background texture for an existing modern premium pizzeria website.
Primary request: an overhead photograph of a very dark smoked wooden pizza-making tabletop with a delicate, sparse dusting of flour. The texture should feel tactile and quietly appetizing, not look like a full scene.
Composition: square 2048x2048, flat top-down surface filling the entire frame, suitable for repeating as a website texture. Fine continuous wood grain, broad understated boards, very faint soft flour smudges and tiny dispersed flour grains, mostly near the outer portions, plenty of calm clean space. All edges have similar dark wood tone so repetition is unobtrusive.
Lighting: soft diffused grazing light, matte finish, understated low contrast.
Palette: warm charcoal, near-black smoked walnut with desaturated taupe grain; flour muted warm grey, no bright white piles.
Constraints: texture only, no food, no pizza, no tools, no props, no hands, no text, no logo, no border, no illustration. Avoid rustic distressed scratches, obvious board seams, large flour piles, dramatic lighting, strong orange wood. Elegant, contemporary, extremely restrained; will be displayed at low opacity behind readable cream typography.
```
